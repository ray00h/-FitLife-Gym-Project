About FitLife Gambia

Your partner in health and fitness since 2018.

Our Story

FitLife Gambia was founded with a mission: to make quality fitness accessible to everyone in The Gambia. What started as a small gym in Serrekunda has grown into one of the county's most trusted fitness centers.

We believe that fitness is not just about building muscles or losing weight. Its about building confindence, improving health and creating a community of people who support each others on their welness journey.

Our Facilities

Our modern facilites offers everything you need for a complete workout:

  • Over 50 pieces of cardio equipment including treadmills, bikes, and ellipticals
  • Full free weight area with dumbbells up to 50kg
  • Dedicated group fitness studio for classes
  • Clean and spacious locker rooms with showers
  • Air-conditioned throughout for your comfort
